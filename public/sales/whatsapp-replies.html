WHATSAPP BUSINESS

Set up once, save hours

  1. Quick replies: Settings → Business tools → Quick replies. Save the first reply as /rates, follow-ups as /follow1 and /follow2, and the booking confirmation as /confirm.
  2. Labels: New → Quoted → Follow-up → Booked / Lost. Check “Quoted” and “Follow-up” every evening.
  3. Greeting message: “Thanks for messaging Workation Dharamshala! I'll reply within a few hours with prices and photos 🏔️”
  4. Catalog: add both stays with photos and “from” prices.
  5. Photos: keep an album of each property (room, work area, view, food) ready to forward.
